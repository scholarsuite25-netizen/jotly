# Antigravity Prompt — Phase 8: Enterprise Collaboration

Implement:
- workspace and department sharing
- comments and review/approval workflow
- action assignment and notification preferences
- invitation and membership lifecycle
- role/permission administration
- SSO integration where selected
- time-limited, revocable cross-organisation share grants only where explicitly supported

Every operation must enforce server-side permissions and produce appropriate audit events. Test role changes, revoked access, expired grants, notification privacy, and tenant boundaries. Do not expose meeting content in notification previews unless policy permits.
